MQS duplicate data: impact on BICoE

Summary as of 8 Oct 2026, for a non-technical audience.

Key messages

  • Three of the five duplicate scenarios reach Silver and Gold. One sample quote now appears three times in Gold.
  • The DVLA retention pipeline is currently failing because of exact duplicates (two copies of a quote with the same timestamp). DVLA (Driver and Vehicle Licensing) data is not being removed on schedule, which is a compliance risk.
  • Reconciliation is not impacted by any of the duplicate scenarios.

1. How MQS data reaches us

BICoE ingests MQS quote data exactly as it is delivered through Event Hub. Every message, including every repeat copy, is written to the landing table. Our duplicate check sits between landing and Bronze, and it only removes copies that are identical (matched on the message header columns) and arrive close together. Any other duplicate passes through to Bronze (DLT tables), Silverish, Silver and Gold. From Bronze, the enrichment tables are loaded and the DLT views are built, and Pricing ART reads those DLT views. Reconciliation is a separate pipeline on the landing table with its own dedup.

3. Impact on Silver and Gold

Example (S2). One quote was first sent on 22 Sep 2026 and resent on 23 and 25 Sep. Dedup removed a same-day copy, but the two later resends passed through. The quote now appears three times in Silver and three times in Gold (the Gold audit layer), once for each day it arrived. Any report that counts quotes by date counts this quote three times.

  • S3 puts conflicting versions of the same quote into Silver and Gold, so it is unclear which one is correct.
  • S4 left historical duplicates in Silver, Gold and enrichment for the backfill period.
  • Pricing ART reads the DLT views built on Bronze, so it is expected to show S2, S3 and S4 duplicates (being checked).

4. The DVLA retention pipeline is failing: a compliance issue

The DVLA retention pipeline is a separate safety check: it scans the landing table and Bronze for unconverted quotes over 60 days old that still hold DVLA data, and removes it. Landing still holds every copy, including the copies that dedup removed before Bronze. Copies of a quote with different timestamps are handled, but when two copies carry exactly the same timestamp, the whole daily run is rejected, and DVLA data is not removed for any quote in that batch.

  • Confirmed for S4 (historical reload) and S5 (exact upstream resends). S1, S2 and S3 do not affect the pipeline.
  • The batch window rolls over 7 days, so one exact duplicate can fail the run several days in a row.
  • A quote whose every eligible run fails keeps its DVLA data, and nothing currently reports it.

5. Good news: reconciliation is not impacted

Reconciliation applies its own duplicate handling and is not affected by any of the five scenarios.
